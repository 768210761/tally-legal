Tally Support

Point your camera at a price tag. Tally reads the amount, works out the country, and shows what it costs in your currency.

Frequently asked

The country is wrong.
Tap the country in the top bar and pick it yourself. Tally remembers the choice. When a tag is ambiguous, every number on it is tappable too.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Scanning, country detection and conversion run on the phone. Only the exchange rate needs the network; without it Tally uses the last saved rates.
How current are the rates?
Rates refresh in the background when you open the app. The rate source and update time are shown under the converted value.
Which countries are supported?
39 countries and regions, each with its own currency and number format.
Where is my data?
On your device. Photos are never uploaded, and there is no account. See the privacy policy.

Tally 支持

把镜头对准价签，Tally 读出金额、判断国家，换成本币给你看。

常见问题

国家判错了
点顶部的国家按钮自己选，Tally 会记住。价签有歧义时，上面的每个数字也都能点。
离线能用吗？
能。拍摄、判国、换算都在手机上完成，只有汇率需要联网；没有网络时会用上一次保存的汇率。
汇率多久更新？
打开 App 时后台更新。汇率来源和更新时间显示在换算结果下方。
支持哪些国家？
39 个国家和地区，各自带货币和数字格式。
我的数据在哪？
只在本机。照片从不上传，也没有账号。详见隐私政策。
