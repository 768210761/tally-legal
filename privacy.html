Tally · Privacy Policy · 2026-11-10

Privacy Policy

Tally reads price tags and converts them into your currency. It is built to work without an account and without sending your photos anywhere.

What stays on your device

Photos you take or pick are processed on the device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. They are never uploaded, and neither are the text or prices read from them. Your ledger is stored locally on your device.

Location

Tally may ask for your approximate location to work out which country you are in. The location is used only on the device, at the moment of a scan, and is not stored or transmitted.

Exchange rates

To keep rates current, Tally fetches public exchange-rate data from open.er-api.com. That request contains no account, no device identifier and no information about you or your scans. If the request fails, Tally keeps working with the last saved rates.

What Tally does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Children

Tally does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

隐私政策

Tally 读取价签并换算成你的货币。它不需要账号，也不会把你的照片发到任何地方。

留在本机的数据

你拍摄或选择的照片，全部使用 Apple 的端侧 Vision 框架在手机上处理，从不上传；从照片里读出的文字和价格也一样。账本只保存在本机。

定位

Tally 可能请求你的大致位置，用来判断你在哪个国家。位置只在扫描的那一刻、在手机本地使用，不保存、不上传。

汇率

为了让汇率保持更新，Tally 会从 open.er-api.com 获取公开汇率数据。这个请求不包含账号、设备标识，也不包含任何与你或你的扫描有关的信息。请求失败时，Tally 会用上一次保存的汇率继续工作。

不做什么

  • 没有账号，不需要登录
  • 没有统计、广告或追踪 SDK
  • 不售卖、不共享个人数据

儿童

Tally 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更

政策如有变化，会在本页发布新版本并更新日期。
